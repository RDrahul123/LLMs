# Northstar Remote Work Policy

## Working hours

Northstar employees may work remotely from locations approved by People Operations. Teams should agree on at least four overlapping working hours each business day. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. in the team's primary time zone.

## Equipment and expenses

New employees receive a company laptop. Employees may request reimbursement for up to $300 per calendar year for ergonomic home-office equipment. Submit receipts through the expense portal within 30 days of purchase. Internet service is not reimbursable.

## Time off

Employees should request planned time off in the HR portal at least five business days in advance. For illness or emergencies, notify your manager as soon as practical. Managers approve time-off requests based on team coverage.
