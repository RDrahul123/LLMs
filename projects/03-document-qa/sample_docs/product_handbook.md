# Northstar Product Handbook

## Product overview

Northstar is a project planning workspace for small teams. The product brings tasks, project notes, and weekly progress updates together in one shared workspace. A project owner can invite teammates, organize tasks into projects, set due dates, and review progress from the dashboard.

## Plans and billing

The Starter plan is free for teams of up to five members and includes three active projects. The Team plan costs $12 per member per month when billed monthly, or $10 per member per month when billed annually. Team includes unlimited projects, project templates, and priority email support. A workspace owner can change a plan from Settings → Billing. Plan changes take effect at the next billing date.

## Tasks and notifications

Tasks can be assigned to one teammate and can have a due date, priority, and description. Users receive an in-app notification when they are assigned a task or mentioned in a project note. Email notifications can be turned off in Personal settings → Notifications. Daily reminder emails are sent at 9:00 a.m. in each user's local time when they have an overdue task.

## Support

Starter customers can contact support at help@northstar.example. Team customers receive priority email support. Support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, excluding public holidays.
